I'm Aishwarya — a Senior Analytics Manager and BI Lead with over 10 years of experience. I care deeply about turning data into decisions and building analytics solutions that drive real business impact.

Before that, I restructured the BI platform at Modern Gourmet Foods, automating 85% of reports and reducing manual effort by 40%. I designed Tableau dashboards and implemented RPA automation that reduced financial data entry time by 98%.

Earlier in my career at TCS (Client: Citi), I designed interactive dashboards used by 100+ global stakeholders to analyze payment trends, user engagement, and transaction volumes — directly informing product roadmap prioritization.

I hold an MS in Information Systems from Cal State Fullerton and am currently deepening my expertise in Snowflake, dbt, and Apache Superset.
